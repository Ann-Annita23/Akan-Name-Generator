The Ghanaian naming culture

The Akan Name Generator

The Akan Name Generator is a simple web tool that generates traditional Ghanaian Akan names based on a person's date of birth and gender. Akan names come from the Akan people of Ghana and are given according to the day of the week a person was born. The generator helps users quickly discover their cultural side.
